Questions owners ask.

What does it cost all in?
$499 once to set up your screen. Then $49 or $79 a month for care, and $149 a month for the Lobby Conversion Engine (2 screens, $199). Restaurants and bars: $999 setup, then $249 a month for Dynamic Dayparting or $149 for Essentials. All month to month.
Can I use the TV I already have?
Yes, that's the default. We check its warranty terms with you before install. Menu boards that run 12 hours a day or more need a commercial display, and we'll tell you if yours does.
What if it freezes?
The player restarts itself every night. On a care plan we check overnight that it's running, and we replace the player if it dies.
Who makes the content?
We do, from your menu, prices, photos, and reviews. To change something, text us.
Do I need Wi-Fi?
Yes. The player pulls updates over your internet connection.
Can the slides be in Spanish?
Yes. We write the slides for the people in your lobby, in English, Spanish, or both.
I have more than two screens.
Restaurants and bars start with the $999 restaurant setup above. Clinics with more than 2 screens get a written quote after the walkthrough.
How fast does it go live?
Dre texts you to set the walkthrough. After that, going live is one in-person install visit.
