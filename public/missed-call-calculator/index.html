Why there are no industry averages here.

Averages from national surveys don't know your phone. A roofer in Van Buren and a med spa in Rogers miss calls for different reasons at different hours.

Your call log does know. So this calculator only uses what you type. If the number it gives you is small, that's a real answer too: start with the free test and fix your greeting.

Will callers know it's AI?
The text and the answering come from your business name. If a caller asks whether they're talking to a person, it tells the truth.

Yes. Every plan works on the number your customers already call.

On the answering plans, emergencies go to a person you name, by call or text.

On an answering plan, your free week includes a Spanish test call on your own line. Billing starts only if that test passes.

The free test runs the first night after Dre confirms your closing time. A plan goes live after one in-person setup visit.
