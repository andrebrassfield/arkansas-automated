Questions owners ask.

Why $997 when your text back plan is $129?
The $129 plan texts back missed calls. That's it. This plan answers, books, confirms, fills cancellations, brings lapsed patients back, and asks for reviews. For a spa whose front desk is dark half the week, booking is the job. If text back is all you need, we'll tell you.
Will callers know it's AI?
It answers as your front desk, in your spa's name. If a caller asks whether she's talking to a person, it tells the truth. It won't lie about what it is.
Do I keep my phone number?
Yes. Your number stays yours. Calls are routed to the front desk from your existing line, and you can switch it back any time.
What about urgent calls, like a patient with a reaction?
Anything clinical or urgent goes straight to the person you name, by call or text. The AI does not give medical advice and does not discuss treatment details.
Does it handle callers in Spanish?
Your free week includes a Spanish test call on your own line. Billing starts only if that test passes.
How fast does it go live?
The free test can run tonight. Going live takes one in-person setup visit. Your free week starts the day it answers real calls.
What about patient privacy?
It books appointments and answers business questions: hours, pricing you approve, directions. It does not discuss treatment details or give medical advice. Clinical questions go to your team.
