Questions owners ask.

Is it really free?
Yes. No card, no contract. You get the recording whether you buy anything or not. If you want the fix, plans start at $129 a month and every price is on this page.
Will my staff know it's a test?
The call comes after you close, so usually nobody's there. If someone does answer, Dre tells them who he is.
Do you record my customers?
No. We record only our own test call to your line.
If I buy a plan, will callers know it's AI?
It answers in your business name. If a caller asks whether they're talking to a person, it tells the truth.
Do I keep my phone number?
Yes. The test uses your number as it is. Every plan keeps your number too.
What about emergencies?
On the answering plans, emergencies go to a person: whoever you name, by call or text. Everything else gets captured and handed off.
Do you handle Spanish-speaking callers?
On an answering plan, your free week includes a Spanish test call on your own line. Billing starts only if that test passes.
How fast does this happen?
The test runs the first night after Dre confirms your closing time. The recording comes back the same day, by the next business morning at the latest. If you buy a plan, going live takes one in-person setup visit.
